# 2) The Brain's 4 Characters (Backbone of the Model)

Taylor explains left/right brain × thinking/emotional circuits as 4 characters. The key point is "who is now at the steering wheel? Recognize → situation-matched teamwork." ([Sloww](https://www.sloww.co/whole-brain-living/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Whole Brain Living by Jill Bolte Taylor (Detailed Book ..."), [Shortform](https://www.shortform.com/blog/jill-bolte-taylor-4-characters/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Jill Bolte Taylor: The 4 Characters in Your Brain That Run ..."))

| Character | Brain Circuit Tendency | Main Desires/Goals | Speech Patterns/Signals (Examples) | Strengths | Risks When Over-Active |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| #1 Left Thinking | Analysis, language, planning | Order, efficiency, achievement | "Checklist first" "What's the deadline?" | Execution, structure | Perfectionism, controlling others |
| #2 Left Emotional | Boundary, risk detection | Safety, belonging, control | "This is a disaster" "Why are they like that?" | Setting boundaries, risk management | Anxiety, anger, defensiveness |
| #3 Right Emotional | Experience, play, empathy | Play, relationships, improvisation | "This sounds fun!" "Feel this now" | Creativity, empathy, engagement | Impulsiveness, scattered, weak boundaries |
| #4 Right Thinking | Big picture, integration | Meaning, equanimity, connection | "Let's pause for a moment" | Insight, compassion, holistic perspective | Reality avoidance, delayed decisions |

> How to Use: When emotions surge, ask "who's at the front now?" (e.g., anger = usually #2, hyperfocus = usually #3). Then invoke #1 and #4 to supplement planning/perspective. ([Sloww](https://www.sloww.co/whole-brain-living/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Whole Brain Living by Jill Bolte Taylor (Detailed Book ..."), [Shortform](https://www.shortform.com/blog/jill-bolte-taylor-4-characters/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Jill Bolte Taylor: The 4 Characters in Your Brain That Run ..."))

# 3) Key Tool ① Brain Huddle

A tool to 'convene' all 4 characters in seconds to 1 minute and choose action by consensus. Here's a practical script. ([Sequence Wiz](https://sequencewiz.org/2023/02/15/how-to-do-the-brain-huddle-to-find-inner-peace/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "How to do the Brain Huddle to find inner peace"), [Shortform](https://www.shortform.com/blog/brain-huddle/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Brain Huddle: Jill Bolte Taylor's Solution to a Cluttered Mind"))

1. **Breathe** – 3 breaths, check body sensation.
2. **Recognize** – "The dominant character right now is #__."
3. **Appreciate** – "Each character has reasons and value."
4. **Inquire** – "What's the best choice now?" (#1 execution vs #4 values vs #3 care vs #2 safety)
5. **Navigate** – Execute one action by consensus (start with a light "first step" of 10 minutes).

> **10-Second Mini Huddle:** "Breathe-label(#2/3/1/4)-one choice." Useful in meetings and conflicts. ([Sequence Wiz](https://sequencewiz.org/2023/02/15/how-to-do-the-brain-huddle-to-find-inner-peace/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "How to do the Brain Huddle to find inner peace"))

# 4) Key Tool ② The 90-Second Rule

The biochemical wave of strong emotion typically subsides within **90 seconds**, and subsequent persistence comes from "thoughts reigniting the loop." That's why breathing, labeling, and rechoosing work together. ([Psychology Today](https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/blog/the-right-mindset/202004/the-90-second-rule-builds-self-control?utm_source=chatgpt.com "The 90-Second Rule That Builds Self-Control"), [Hintsa](https://www.hintsa.com/insights/blogs/improve-your-emotional-self-awareness/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Improve Your Emotional Self-Awareness With This 90- ..."))

# 5) Situation-Specific Application Checklist

**A. Conflict/Anger Response (Before sending message, meeting, comment)**

- Check physical signals (heart rate, heat sensation) → Mini huddle (10 seconds) → "Delayed response" template (#1): *'That's a good point. I'll reply by tomorrow morning.'* → Rewrite from #4 perspective after 12-24 hours. ([Shortform](https://www.shortform.com/blog/brain-huddle/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Brain Huddle: Jill Bolte Taylor's Solution to a Cluttered Mind"))

**B. Anxiety/Fear (#2 Over-activation) Relief**

- Thank #2, "Your goal is safety, right?" → #1 to *one immediately possible step* ('Today just budget overview') → #4 to reconfirm purpose ('maintaining relationships/health'). ([Shortform](https://www.shortform.com/blog/jill-bolte-taylor-4-characters/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Jill Bolte Taylor: The 4 Characters in Your Brain That Run ..."))

**C. Hyperfocus/Impulse (#3 Over-activation) Control**

- Set 15-minute timer, block distractions (app blocker) → #1 checklist 3 items only → After completion, give #3 a '10-minute reward'. ([Sloww](https://www.sloww.co/whole-brain-living/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Whole Brain Living by Jill Bolte Taylor (Detailed Book ..."))

**D. Relationships and Teamwork**

- 30-second huddle before meeting (team rule: breathe–label–one agreement today) → At finish, confirm from #4 perspective "meaning of this meeting/one next action." ([Koya Culture](https://www.koya-culture.com/news/article.html?no=136127&utm_source=chatgpt.com "Study Neuroscience to Understand Yourself"))

# 8) Strengths and Cautions (Scientific Context)

- **Strengths:** Translates emotion/behavior control into 'circuit language' and provides **immediately actionable routines** (4-character model + huddle + 90 seconds). ([Sloww](https://www.sloww.co/whole-brain-living/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Whole Brain Living by Jill Bolte Taylor (Detailed Book ..."), [Shortform](https://www.shortform.com/blog/brain-huddle/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Brain Huddle: Jill Bolte Taylor's Solution to a Cluttered Mind"))
- **Caution:** The popular 'left-brain=reason/right-brain=emotion' **dominance myth** has been criticized as oversimplified/misinterpreted. In reality, there is **relative localization** of functions, and most tasks involve both hemispheres cooperating. This book's framework is practical as a **self-understanding and self-regulation tool**, but it's safer not to misunderstand it as neuroscience-based personality determinism. ([Scientific American](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/5-common-myths-about-the-brain/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "5 Common Myths about the Brain"), [Harvard Health](https://www.health.harvard.edu/blog/right-brainleft-brain-right-2017082512222?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Right brain/left brain, right?"))
