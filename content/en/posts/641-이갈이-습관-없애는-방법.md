Teeth grinding (bruxism) is the habit of unconsciously grinding or clenching your teeth during sleep. It has various causes and solutions. I'll explain comprehensively, including the latest information.

## How to Eliminate Teeth Grinding Habits

Teeth grinding is not a simple habit but a sleep disorder that occurs from complex causes. Effective improvement requires identifying the underlying causes and taking various approaches.

### Main Causes

The cause of teeth grinding is still not clearly understood, but currently it's understood as a phenomenon related to stress or the central nervous system. The most common cause of teeth grinding is arousal disorder during sleep.

### Treatment Methods

#### 1. **Occlusal Splint (Mouth Guard)**

An occlusal splint prevents damage to teeth by preventing the upper and lower molars from touching.

**Precaution**: Relying solely on a teeth grinding mouth guard won't provide fundamental treatment. While the mouth guard is effective in protecting teeth, it doesn't eliminate teeth grinding itself.

#### 2. **Botox Treatment**

Injecting botox into the jaw muscles reduces biting force, which can reduce problems caused by teeth grinding. The effect lasts about 6 months and also improves jaw squareness.

#### 3. **Sleep Breathing Disorder Treatment**

Approximately 85% of teeth grinding patients are related to sleep breathing disorders. If you have snoring or sleep apnea, you should consider positive airway pressure therapy.

### Lifestyle Improvements

#### 1. **Change Sleep Position**

Most teeth grinding patients find that teeth grinding doesn't occur when they take certain sleep positions. Just changing sleep positions, such as sleeping on your side instead of lying on your back, can significantly alleviate teeth grinding symptoms.

#### 2. **Stress Management**

- Meditation, yoga, deep breathing exercises
- Regular exercise to reduce stress hormones
- Before bed, warm a towel and wrap it from the cheeks to the jaw, gently massaging the facial, neck, and jaw muscles to relax the jaw joint

#### 3. **Lifestyle Correction**

Excessive drinking and smoking are reported to increase the incidence of teeth grinding by up to 5 times.

**Habits to avoid**:

- Excessive smoking and drinking
- Excessive caffeine consumption
- Resting your chin on your hand, biting nails or pencils
- Frequently chewing gum or tough foods
- Teeth clenching during the day

### Importance of Diagnosis

**Key Point**: Before treating teeth grinding, it's advisable to identify the cause through polysomnography, a comprehensive sleep test, and apply the most appropriate treatment method. Especially, it's important to check whether sleep breathing disorders are present.

### Key Points Easy to Overlook

1. **Teeth grinding is a 'disease'**: It's not a simple bad habit but a sleep disorder requiring treatment.
2. **Checking for accompanying conditions is essential**: You must check if there are sleep breathing disorders like snoring or sleep apnea.
3. **Long-term perspective is necessary**: Currently, there's no treatment that can fundamentally eliminate teeth grinding. Focus should be on symptom management and prevention.
4. **Check family history**: Teeth grinding may have genetic predisposition, so check if family members have teeth grinding.

If teeth grinding symptoms persist or are severe, it's important to visit a sleep clinic or specialist in dental medicine to receive accurate diagnosis and customized treatment.
