## Overview

The Livelihood Recovery Consumer Voucher is the first nationwide consumer support policy promoted by the Lee government to boost economic demand and recover household economics[1]. The second supplementary budget of 31.8 trillion won, which was voted on in the National Assembly on July 4, 2025, includes a 12.1709 trillion won budget for the Livelihood Recovery Consumer Voucher[2][3].

## Payment Schedule and Amount

### First Payment (July 21 - September 12)

The first payment targets **all citizens**, with the payment standard date being June 18, 2025, based on residents currently living in South Korea[1][4].

**Basic Payment Amount**[5][6]:

- **General Public**: 150,000 won per person
- **Near-Poor Families and Single-Parent Families**: 300,000 won per person
- **Basic Living Allowance Recipients**: 400,000 won per person

**Regional Additional Support**[5][6]:

- **Non-Capital Area** (excluding Seoul, Gyeonggi, Incheon): Additional 30,000 won
- **Rural Areas with Declining Population** (84 cities and counties): Additional 50,000 won

As a result, **the maximum amount an individual can receive is 450,000 won**[4].

### Second Payment (September 22 - October 31)

The second payment targets **90% of citizens excluding the top 10% by income based on health insurance contributions**, providing an additional **100,000 won per person**[6][7]. Specific eligibility criteria will be announced in September[6].

## Application Method

### Application Period

- **First Application**: July 21, 2025, 9:00 AM - September 12, 6:00 PM[1][4]
- **Application Deadline**: Must apply within the period; failure to apply results in forfeiture of funds[8]

### Eligibility

- **Adults** (born before December 31, 2006): Individual application[7]
- **Minors** (born on or after January 1, 2007): Head of household applies on their behalf[9]

### Application Methods

**1. Credit/Debit Cards**[7]:

- **Online**: Card company homepage, app, call center, ARS
- **Offline**: Visit bank branch linked to the card
- **Payment**: Automatic charge the day after application

**2. Regional Love Gift Certificates**[7]:

- **Online**: Regional Love Gift Certificate app or homepage managed by local government
- **Offline**: Visit eup/myeon/dong administrative welfare center (paper form)
- **Payment**: Provided the day after application

**3. Prepaid Card**[7]:

- **Offline**: Visit eup/myeon/dong administrative welfare center
- **Payment**: Receive on-site immediately

### Day-of-Week System (First Week of Application)

To prevent application overload, a day-of-week system applies based on birth year[10][7]:

- **Monday**: 1, 6
- **Tuesday**: 2, 7
- **Wednesday**: 3, 8
- **Thursday**: 4, 9
- **Friday**: 5, 0
- **Weekend**: All citizens can apply

## Usage Guide

### Usage Period

**Valid until November 30, 2025**; unused balances after this date will be forfeited to the state and local governments[11][6].

### Usage Region

**Available only within the local government jurisdiction of registered residence**[11]:

- **Metropolitan City/Special City Residents**: Within that city
- **Provincial Residents**: Within the relevant city or county

### Eligible Venues

**Allowed**[5][12]:

- Traditional markets, neighborhood marts, convenience stores
- Restaurants, cafes, bakeries
- Hair salons, eyeglass shops
- Academies, clinics, pharmacies
- Businesses with annual sales of 3 billion won or less
- Hanarochom (with exceptions for 125 locations in rural areas)

**Not Allowed**[5][12]:

- Large discount stores, large supermarkets
- Department stores, duty-free shops
- Online shopping malls, delivery apps
- Entertainment establishments, gambling businesses
- Cash-convertible businesses

## Special Information

### Pre-notification Service

Starting July 14, users can subscribe to pre-notification service through 17 mobile apps including Naver, KakaoTalk, and Toss, and the National Secretary homepage to receive notifications about individual payment amounts and application methods starting July 19[1][13].

### Foreign National Payment Criteria

Foreign nationals are generally excluded but may exceptionally receive payment if[14][13]:

- Listed on a resident registration with at least one Korean national and enrolled in health insurance
- Green card holder (F-5), Marriage migrant (F-6), Refugee (F-2-4) with health insurance enrollment

### Overseas Residents

Citizens who returned from overseas between June 18 and September 12, can receive vouchers after confirming entry/exit facts and filing objections[15][16].

### Objections

Those with objections to eligibility or payment amount can file objections through the Civil Complaint Portal or administrative welfare centers from July 21 to September 12[11].

### Security Precautions

The government and card companies will not send text messages containing URLs related to the Livelihood Recovery Consumer Voucher, so users are advised never to click links from unknown sources[11][7].

## Policy Objectives

The Livelihood Recovery Consumer Voucher is an **exceptional measure to recover household economy**, supporting expanded sales for small business owners and the self-employed through overall consumption activation while simultaneously pursuing income support for those in need[1][6]. The total required funds amount to 13.9 trillion won, comprising 12.2 trillion won in national funds and 1.7 trillion won in local funds[1].
