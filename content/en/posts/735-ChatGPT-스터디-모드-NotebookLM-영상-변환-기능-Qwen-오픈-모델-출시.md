**Today, July 29, 2025 (July 30 in Korea), three significant advances have emerged in the AI education sector. ChatGPT has strengthened its educator role with 'Study Mode', Google's NotebookLM has added the ability to convert text to video, and Alibaba's Qwen3-30B-A3B-2507 small model has showcased an innovative feature that allows seamless switching between thinking and conversation modes.**

## ChatGPT Study Mode: AI Becomes Your Personal Tutor

On July 29, 2025, OpenAI officially launched 'Study Mode' for ChatGPT. Rather than simply providing answers, this feature guides students through solving problems step by step. Study Mode employs the Socratic method to stimulate students' thinking processes and reduces their dependence on direct answers.

**Key Features and How It Works**

When Study Mode is activated, ChatGPT provides guided questions adjusted to match the student's goals and skill level. If a student requests a direct answer, ChatGPT informs them that solving it independently is a better learning approach. Users can toggle Study Mode on or off at any point during conversation, still maintaining easy access to answers.

**Reactions from Educators and Broader Implications**

In testing by Common Sense Media, when asking regular ChatGPT to write an answer about "To Kill a Mockingbird" like a 9th grader, it complied verbatim. Study Mode, however, responded with "I won't write it for you, but let's work through it together!" This demonstrates AI's clear philosophy of supporting student learning without doing the work for them.

## NotebookLM Video Overviews: Documents Transform Into Videos

On the same day, Google launched the 'Video Overviews' feature in NotebookLM. This feature converts complex documents, PDFs, and images into easily digestible visual presentations. As a visual alternative to the existing audio overview, AI generates new visual materials while drawing images, diagrams, quotations, and numbers from the uploaded documents to explain content.

**Technical Innovation and Potential Applications**

NotebookLM leverages Gemini 2.5 Pro's multimodal capabilities to convert PDFs, images, text, webpages, and YouTube videos uploaded by users into animated shorts for intuitive summaries and explanations. It can analyze up to 50 sources (up to 500,000 words per source) and generate clips 5-15 minutes in length, complete with comic-style visual effects, dynamic text, and AI narration.

**Real-World Performance and Limitations**

According to AIbase's testing, when a 100-page PDF was uploaded, a 10-minute video could be generated in just 5 minutes with 90% accuracy, including core concepts, chart analysis, and quotations. Currently, it supports English only, though support for other languages is planned.

## Qwen3-30B-A3B-2507: AI That Freely Switches Between Thinking and Conversation

Qwen3-30B-A3B-2507, developed by Alibaba Cloud's Qwen team, is a MoE (Mixture of Experts) model with a total of 30 billion parameters, though only 3 billion parameters are actually activated. Its most distinctive feature is seamless switching between 'thinking mode' for complex logical reasoning, mathematics, and coding, and 'non-thinking mode' for efficient general-purpose conversation.

**Performance and Efficiency**

Despite being a small MoE model, Qwen3-30B-A3B outperforms QwQ-32B, which has 10 times more active parameters. Even smaller models like Qwen3-4B match the performance of Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct. The thinking model uses temperature 0.6 and top_p 0.95, while the instruction model uses temperature 0.7 and top_p 0.8, with each optimized differently.

**Open-Source Accessibility**

Two MoE models and six dense models are provided as open-source under the Apache 2.0 license, enabling researchers, developers, and organizations to build innovative solutions leveraging state-of-the-art models.

## Long-Term Impact and the Future of Education

These three technologies present a new paradigm for AI in education. **Personalized learning experiences** have emerged as the core keyword, with AI evolving from a simple answer provider to a true learning partner.

**In terms of educational equity**, these technologies are a double-edged sword. They provide access to a tool that can replace expensive private tutors, but simultaneously carry the risk of providing inaccurate information. The fact that students can still easily switch to normal mode creates potential for new forms of "smart cheating."

## Practical Strategies for Educators and Learners

I propose three strategies to respond to these technological trends:

• **Gradual Integration**: Rather than completely replacing existing educational methods, utilize these as complementary tools and design curricula focused on developing students' critical thinking skills

• **Ethical Use Education**: Cultivate learners' ability to make independent judgments through systematic education on proper use and limitations of AI tools

• **Evaluation Method Innovation**: Establish new systems that evaluate thinking processes and creative problem-solving abilities rather than mere memorization or finding correct answers

## Technology Glossary (Explained for Everyone)

**Socratic Method**: Instead of a teacher directly giving answers, this method guides students to find answers themselves through questioning. It's like gradually giving hints when solving a riddle.

**MoE (Mixture of Experts)**: Like multiple experts each handling only their specialty, this is a technique where only parts of an AI model are activated to operate efficiently.

**Multimodal**: The ability to simultaneously understand and process multiple types of information including text, images, voice, and video.

**Parameters**: Units where AI stores learned information. The larger the number, the more knowledge it can be said to possess. It's a concept similar to neural connections in the human brain.
