1. When are you happy being with someone?
2. When are you unhappy being with someone?
3. When do you grow being with someone?
4. Even if they are your perfect ideal type, what are 3 things you absolutely cannot tolerate?
5. What are others' shortcomings you can overlook even if someone isn't great?
6. What's okay about being different from your partner?
7. What would you like to be the same?
8. What is the most important value in your life?

**To increase intimacy with your partner**

- Open and deep self-disclosure
- The "me first, then you" communication style
- Going to places where good people gather
- Building good energy (self-esteem, confidence, physical health, mental management)
- Become a necessary person
- Practice being a giver
- Speak with precise expression at the right timing (reducing misunderstandings)
