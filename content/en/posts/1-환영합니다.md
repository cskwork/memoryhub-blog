**#2 There are many different skins available.**

![](/images/1-환영합니다/welcome2.jpg)

Explore the various '[skins](https://www.tistory.com/skin)' on Tistory.   
You can choose a skin that matches the purpose of your blog or website.   
What kind of content will you be sharing mainly? Think carefully and choose the skin you like.  
You can create a great-looking homepage by simply '[editing the skin cover](/manage/design/skin/edit#/)', so try it out!
