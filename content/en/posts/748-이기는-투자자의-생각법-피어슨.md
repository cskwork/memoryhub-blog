# Chapter 1. All Investing is Timing

**Core insight:** "Profits are made by **timing and risk assumptions**, not technique."

- Key concepts
  - **Overconfidence = Crisis signal**: Beware confirmation bias of "I succeeded with my skill."
  - **Timing > Analysis**: Analysis is not sufficient. **Entry and exit rules** are necessary.
  - **Buying is a skill, selling is an art**: Selling considers profit/loss, psychology, and alternative assets.
- Practical checklist
  1. Pre-fill 3 worst-case scenarios (rising rates, declining income, liquidity squeeze)
  2. **Document** entry conditions (price/fundamentals/liquidity) **AND** stop-loss and rebalancing conditions
  3. Set maximum loss per position (e.g., 1-2% of total assets)
  4. Exit triggers: target price, stop-loss level, event risk—each independent
  5. Post-trade **review note**: separate intent, outcome, and luck (external variables)
- Common traps: Delaying stops to avoid losses, rushing profit-taking and limiting winners.

# Chapter 2. Understand Currency (Credit Money, Petrodollar, CBDC, AI/Metaverse Links, Gold vs. Real Estate)

**Core insight:** "**Understand the currency system**, and you see the big waves of assets."

- Key concepts
  - **Credit money**: Supplied by central bank/bank balance expansion—cascades to inflation and asset prices.
  - **Petrodollar system**: Dollar's primacy in oil settlement **sustains dollar demand**.
  - **CBDC/Digital payment**: Potential to **shorten policy transmission paths** via payment data.
  - **Asset selection**: Compare **gold and real estate hedging** characteristics during inflation phases.
- Practical checklist
  1. Weekly check **relationships between dollar index, WTI, and interest rates**
  2. Subscribe to **domestic payment and settlement policy updates** (= CBDC momentum)
  3. Assign **minimum real hedges (gold/REITs/infrastructure)** to portfolio
  4. When choosing hedged/unhedged ETFs, **consider the currency cycle**
  5. "Terminology→real feeling" loop: Record **life clues** at gas stations, exchange offices, import prices
- Common traps: Treating crypto/gold/real estate as **zero-sum substitutes** (different contexts and purposes).

# Chapter 3. Use Leverage as a Tool (Understanding Leverage and Liquidity)

**Core insight:** "Leverage gives you **speed**, but when **liquidity** stops, it becomes a knife."

- Key concepts
  - **Good debt vs. bad debt**: Select only debt **repayable from cash flow**.
  - **Maturity, interest rate, repayment structure**: Fixed/floating, maturity mismatch is key risk.
  - **Liquidity cycle**: Credit expansion → price rise → regulatory tightening → contraction loop.
- Practical checklist
  1. Test **repayment capacity** with DSR/DSCR and stress interest rates (e.g., +150bp)
  2. Review **variable→mixed (partial fixed) risk dispersion**
  3. Create **maturity rollover calendar** (quarterly exposure)
  4. Simulate **cash flow volatility** by period (tenant/dividend variations)
  5. Lower LTV target (5-10%p buffer) to **force safety margin**
- Common traps: All-in leverage on rate-cut assumptions, concentrated maturity.

# Chapter 4. Interest Rates and Exchange Rates Determine Investment Timing (Real Estate as a Bond Proxy)

**Core insight:** "**Interest rate direction and speed** and **exchange rate level** account for 80% of timing."

- Key concepts
  - **Interest rate → exchange rate → inflation** cascade; dollar strength pressures emerging market assets.
  - **Real estate = long-duration asset**: Structural correlation with bond rates.
  - **Country and sector sensitivity differences**: Export vs. import stocks, domestic vs. export city temperature gaps.
- Practical checklist
  1. **Policy meeting calendar** (FOMC, monetary committee) and **dot plot/forward guidance** summary
  2. Set **USDKRW band** (e.g., 1200-1400), reduce position when broken
  3. Watch for **rate-cut signals** (10Y sovereign yield, inflation leading indicators) to **extend duration**
  4. Pre-create **real estate/stock/bond allocation table** by interest rate/FX scenario
  5. Sub-monitor commodities and freight indices (supply-side inflation)
- Common traps: "Ignore levels" entry (long maturities during high-rate/high-FX regimes).

# Chapter 5. Real Estate Investing in Practice (Fair Price Calculation, Cases, Worst-Case Assumptions, Trend Inference)

**Core insight:** "**Fair price calculation + worst-case assumptions + trend inference** = survival-mode purchase."

- Key concepts
  - **Fair purchase price**: Calculated from regional value, rent, interest rates, alternatives (jeonse/monthly).
  - **Case studies**: **Cash flow analysis** includes transaction costs, taxes, vacancy rate, repairs.
  - **Trend inference**: Cross-check **price, transaction volume, unsold inventory, lending rates**.
- Practical checklist
  1. ①Usable area/location ②Rental/sales spread ③Effective yield → create **intrinsic value table**
  2. Compare using **after-tax returns** (acquisition, holding, sale taxes)
  3. Stress-test with **3-month vacancy and one repair cycle**
  4. Calculate **IRR range** for interest rate scenarios (±100bp/−50bp)
  5. Before signing, write and sign a **1-page risk acknowledgment** as psychological commitment
- Common traps: Overestimating monthly-to-sale conversion, omitting actual repair/common reserve costs.

# Chapter 6. Blend Investing into Your Life (Distinguish Investment from Speculation, Embrace Uncertainty, Gamify)

**Core insight:** "Investing lasts when you **align it with your life's rhythm**."

- Key concepts
  - **Investment vs. speculation**: Distinguish by evidence, duration, cash flow, and presence of risk management.
  - **Gamification**: Routine, logging, and review systems create **sustainability**.
  - **Uncertainty affinity**: Volatility = opportunity, but **minimize bankruptcy probability** by design.
- Practical checklist
  1. Weekly 30-minute **review time**: separate profits, process, and luck factors
  2. **Rule card (1 page)**: print entry, exit, risk, and logging rules—keep on desk
  3. Manage **health, relationships, learning** KPIs (sleep, exercise, reading) **alongside** profit KPIs
  4. Designate "no-touch days" (prevent overtrading)
  5. **Rule refactoring once yearly**: data-driven improvements
- Common traps: Frequent rule changes based on short-term results → consistency collapse.

# Chapter 7. Investor Mindset in the Trump Era (Tariffs and Dollar Strength Scenarios)

**Core insight:** "Policy shocks (tariffs, regulation) shake **dollars, inflation, and trade flows**."

- Key concepts
  - **Decision-making style**: Issue-focused, tariff/regulation **surprise risk**.
  - **Tariffs = cost-push**: Potential **simultaneous shock** to import prices, corporate margins, exchange rates.
  - **Korea's exposure path**: US export share, component chains, won sensitivity.
- Practical checklist
  1. **Monitor China/global tariff news** with USDKRW and KOSPI **simultaneously**
  2. **Tactically adjust** export vs. domestic stock weights (prefer domestic during tariff hikes)
  3. Create **tariff-sensitive sector risk map** (commodities, semiconductors, autos)
  4. During **strong-dollar scenarios**, increase hedges, cash, and short-duration holdings
  5. **Lighten positions** and defend cash flow when policy uncertainty expands
- Common traps: Chase-enter into **volatility spikes** before and after events.

## Appendix: 10-Minute "Timing Routine" (Weekly)

1. Macro: FOMC/monetary committee calendar & **10Y yield/inflation indicators** changes
2. Dollar-won: USDKRW **band breaks**
3. Liquidity: Credit spread and loan balance direction
4. Assets: **Re-check worst scenario** for your portfolio (loss ceiling, cash allocation)
5. Review: Record **one mistake this week** and assign one corrective action

**One-line conclusion:** *Structured routines that read timing and discipline that prevents overconfidence—these are the winning investor's mindset!* ✦
