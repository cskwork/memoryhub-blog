Breaking free from perfectionism is not an easy process, but you can create gradual change through the following methods:

**1. Find the Root of Perfectionism:**

- Think about where your **perfectionism originates**. It may come from **parental influence**, past experiences, etc.
- When you understand the **root of perfectionism**, you can view it objectively and change it.

**2. Set Realistic Goals:**

- Rather than pursuing **perfect results**, set **realistic and achievable goals**.
- Focus on breaking goals into smaller steps and achieving them one by one.

**3. Allow Mistakes:**

- Accept that **mistakes are opportunities for growth and learning**.
- When you make mistakes, find lessons to learn rather than blaming yourself.
- Tell yourself "I don't need to be perfect. **Mistakes are natural.**"

**4. Accept Yourself As You Are:**

- Acknowledge and accept both your **strengths and weaknesses**.
- Remember that **your worth** is not determined by achievements or results.
- Tell yourself "I am **worthy and deserve love.**"

**5. Enjoy the Process:**

- Try to **enjoy the process itself** rather than obsessing over results.
- Don't miss the **joy of the process** while pursuing **perfect results**.

**6. Free Yourself from Others' Judgments:**

- Don't get too caught up in other people's expectations or evaluations.
- Act according to **your values and standards**.
- Tell yourself "My life is my own story. I don't need to be **swayed by others' views.**"

**7. Seek Professional Help:**

- If it's difficult to overcome perfectionism alone, consider seeking help from a **professional counselor** or **therapist**.
- **Professionals** can help you find the root of perfectionism and develop healthy thinking patterns.

**8. Practice Self-Compassion:**

- Speak to yourself as you would to a friend or family member, with **warm and encouraging words**.
- Practice **acknowledging and accepting** your shortcomings.
- Comfort yourself: "It's okay, you're doing well enough. **You don't need to be perfect.**"

**9. Set Priorities:**

- Rather than trying to do everything **perfectly**, **prioritize important tasks**.
- For **lower-priority tasks**, do them adequately or ask for help if needed.

**10. Lower Your Definition of 'Perfect':**

- Instead of 100% **perfection**, try to **be satisfied** with about 80% quality.
- **Lowering standards** often doesn't significantly reduce the quality of results.

**11. Practice Positive Self-Talk:**

- Recognize **negative self-talk caused by perfectionism** and practice changing it to positive.
- Praise yourself: "This is **good enough.**"

**12. Observe Others' Imperfections:**

- Notice that **others around you are also imperfect**.
- Just as you accept their imperfections, you can **accept your own**.

**13. Take Time for Rest and Self-Care:**

- Perfectionism can cause stress and burnout. You need adequate **rest and self-care**.
- Take **time for yourself** and rejuvenate your mind and body.

**14. Use Your Perfectionist Tendencies As Strengths:**

- Rather than completely eliminating perfectionist tendencies, find ways to **use them as strengths**.
- For example, **high standards** can help improve quality. Just be careful not to overdo it.

**15. Celebrate Change:**

- Acknowledge and celebrate **your efforts to break free from perfectionism**.
- Give **meaning to small changes and achievements** and encourage yourself.

**16. Recognize the Cost of Perfectionism:**

- Think about how perfectionism affects your life. You may experience **stress, anxiety, relationship problems**, etc.
- Recognizing the **cost of perfectionism** can motivate change.

**17. Try New Experiences:**

- Step out of familiar and safe territory and **try new experiences**.
- Through new experiences, you can learn that **being imperfect is okay**.

**18. Recall Past Success Stories:**

- Think about **experiences that were successful even though they weren't perfect**.
- Through this, you can realize that **perfectionism is not a necessary condition for success**.

**19. Experiment by Challenging Perfectionism:**

- Intentionally **conduct small experiments that challenge your perfectionism**.
- For example, deliberately make **minor mistakes** or **submit assignments** at the 80% level.
- Through these experiments, you can directly experience the **irrationality of perfectionism**.

**20. Find Your Own Values:**

- To break free from perfectionism, it's important to **find your own values**.
- Besides achievements or success, think about **what is truly important to you**.
- As you live based on **your values**, you can become **free from perfectionism's pressure**.

Breaking free from perfectionism doesn't happen overnight. It requires a **process of understanding and accepting yourself**. **Celebrate small changes** and **be patient with yourself**. **Remember that you can love yourself even with imperfections. You are already precious and lovely exactly as you are.**
