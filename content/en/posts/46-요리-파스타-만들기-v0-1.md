### **1 Ingredient Preparation**

![](/images/46-%EC%9A%94%EB%A6%AC-%ED%8C%8C%EC%8A%A4%ED%83%80-%EB%A7%8C%EB%93%A4%EA%B8%B0-v0-1/img.jpg)

![](/images/46-%EC%9A%94%EB%A6%AC-%ED%8C%8C%EC%8A%A4%ED%83%80-%EB%A7%8C%EB%93%A4%EA%B8%B0-v0-1/img_1.jpg)

![](/images/46-%EC%9A%94%EB%A6%AC-%ED%8C%8C%EC%8A%A4%ED%83%80-%EB%A7%8C%EB%93%A4%EA%B8%B0-v0-1/img_2.jpg)

Prepare half an onion and 5 cloves of garlic sliced.  
(Slice onion like fried rice, garlic thinly)

Also slice the sausage.  
(You can just slit it, but the flavor is too strong so slice it small)

Also slice the bacon small.  
>Prepare onion, garlic, mushroom, bacon, sausage

Add water to a pot and add salt.  
Water 1000:Noodles 100:Salt 10

### **2 Making Stir-Fry Ingredients**

![](/images/46-%EC%9A%94%EB%A6%AC-%ED%8C%8C%EC%8A%A4%ED%83%80-%EB%A7%8C%EB%93%A4%EA%B8%B0-v0-1/img_3.jpg)

![](/images/46-%EC%9A%94%EB%A6%AC-%ED%8C%8C%EC%8A%A4%ED%83%80-%EB%A7%8C%EB%93%A4%EA%B8%B0-v0-1/img_4.jpg)

![](/images/46-%EC%9A%94%EB%A6%AC-%ED%8C%8C%EC%8A%A4%ED%83%80-%EB%A7%8C%EB%93%A4%EA%B8%B0-v0-1/img_5.jpg)

Add onion and garlic and stir-fry.

After frying a bit, add mushroom, sausage, and bacon and stir-fry.

When everything is cooked and the color darkens (?) it's done.

### **3 Boiling Pasta Noodles**

![](/images/46-%EC%9A%94%EB%A6%AC-%ED%8C%8C%EC%8A%A4%ED%83%80-%EB%A7%8C%EB%93%A4%EA%B8%B0-v0-1/img_6.jpg)

![](/images/46-%EC%9A%94%EB%A6%AC-%ED%8C%8C%EC%8A%A4%ED%83%80-%EB%A7%8C%EB%93%A4%EA%B8%B0-v0-1/img_7.jpg)

![](/images/46-%EC%9A%94%EB%A6%AC-%ED%8C%8C%EC%8A%A4%ED%83%80-%EB%A7%8C%EB%93%A4%EA%B8%B0-v0-1/img_8.jpg)

One serving of noodles is the size of a coin when held in hand  
(It looks like less than you'd think, but it has a lot of volume when boiled)

Drop the noodles into rapidly boiling water.  
Chewy pasta 7 minutes  
Regular al dente 8 minutes  
Soft pasta 9 minutes
