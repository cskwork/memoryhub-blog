## 1. Background - Why Clear Instructions Matter

AI models use natural language processing to communicate humanly, but subtle differences in prompt wording significantly impact AI interpretation and output quality.

### 🔍 Impact of clarity on results

According to a 2025 AI Impact Lab survey, 54% reported noticeably improved content quality when using structured prompt templates.

**Problems with vague instructions:**

- More AI guesses needed ↑ = Unwanted results ↑
- Multiple interpretations possible = Inconsistent output
- Lack of context = Generic, superficial answers

## 2. Core Concepts - CLEAR Framework

The CLEAR framework includes 5 core principles for effective AI content generation.

> **CLEAR = 5-step formula for clear conversation with AI**

### 🎯 CLEAR Components Explained

**C - Concise**

- Remove unnecessary words
- Deliver only essentials

**L - Logical**

- Sequential structure
- Clear cause-and-effect relationships

**E - Explicit**

- Direct expression over implication
- State specific requirements

**A - Adaptive**

- Reflect feedback
- Improve iteratively

**R - Reflective**

- Evaluate results
- Refine prompts

## 3. Hands-On - 7 Techniques to Improve Clarity

### ① Add Specific Details

Vague prompts often result in generic or irrelevant responses.

**❌ Vague Example:**

```
"Tell me about marketing strategy"
```

**✅ Clear Example:**

```
"Provide an SNS marketing strategy for an eco-friendly cosmetics 
brand targeting South Korean women in their 20s in 2025, with 
specific content examples for each of 3 channels: Instagram, TikTok, 
and YouTube"
```

### ② Provide Context and Background

AI doesn't automatically know who your audience is or what your project is. Include important background details.

```
# Prompt with context
"Context: We're a B2B SaaS startup targeting HR managers at SMEs.

Task: Write an email explaining our product's 3 main features 
(employee performance management tool)

Tone: Professional yet friendly
Length: Under 300 words"
```

### ③ Specify Output Format

**Format specification example:**

```
"Please answer in the following format:

## Title: [Key Message]

### 1. Problem Definition (2-3 sentences)
- Current situation
- Problem points

### 2. Solutions (Max 3 lines each)
- Solution A: [Explanation]
- Solution B: [Explanation]

### 3. Expected Results (Expressed numerically)
- Result 1: XX% improvement
- Result 2: XX hours saved"
```

### ④ Specify Do's and Don'ts

Telling AI what you want and don't want in responses saves time and improves results.

```
"When writing customer service responses:

✅ Include:
- Empathetic language
- Specific solutions
- Next steps guidance

❌ Avoid:
- Jargon
- Responsibility disclaimers
- Vague time expressions (e.g., soon, later)"
```

### ⑤ Break Into Steps

Instead of requesting a large project at once, divide it into smaller, focused steps.

```
"Help me write a blog post:

Step 1: Identify 3 major issues on 'AI Ethics'
Step 2: Organize pro and con arguments for each
Step 3: Develop a balanced conclusion
Step 4: Draft a 500-word outline"
```

### ⑥ Provide Examples (Few-Shot)

```
"Write product descriptions in this style:

Example 1:
Product: Wireless earbuds
Description: "All-day comfort, all-night battery - add the perfect 
soundtrack to your daily life"

Example 2:
Product: Smartwatch
Description: "More than telling time - a reliable partner for a 
healthier lifestyle"

Now describe our product:
Product: Air purifier
Description: [Write here]"
```

### ⑦ Set Constraints

Clear constraints help AI cut through the mountain of possibilities and provide more focused, useful output.

```
"Instagram caption requirements:
- Length: Under 100 characters
- Emojis: 3 or fewer
- Hashtags: 5 total (3 in Korean, 2 in English)
- Tone: Casual, 20s sensibility
- Forbidden words: Best, greatest, perfect"
```

## 4. Best Practices - Before & After Comparison

### 📊 Real improvement examples

| Situation | Before (Vague) | After (Clear) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Report request** | "Make a sales report" | "Compile Q4 2024 online channel sales with quarter-over-quarter change rates in a table, then analyze the performance factors of TOP 3 products" |
| **Email writing** | "Write a rejection email" | "Write a polite rejection email for the collaboration proposal. Include: thank you message + explanation of current resource constraints + keeping the door open for future opportunities. Under 150 words" |
| **Content planning** | "SNS content ideas" | "5 Instagram Reel content ideas for café owners in their 1st year. 15 seconds each, educational yet entertaining" |

### ✅ Clarity Checklist

After writing a prompt, check:

- **WHO**: Is the audience/target clear?
- **WHAT**: Is the desired output specific?
- **WHEN**: Is time context stated if applicable?
- **WHERE**: Is the usage context/platform specified?
- **WHY**: Is the purpose clear?
- **HOW**: Are format/style/tone specified?

## 5. Closing Thoughts

Writing clear prompts isn't just a skill. It helps to think of prompts as "programming with words."

**✨ 3-Line Summary**

- Specificity wins: State details, context, and format
- Examples have power: Provide samples of desired style
- Iteration perfects: Don't settle on first attempt—keep improving

**💡 Real-world tip:** Templatize frequently-used prompts and share with your team for consistent, quality results!
