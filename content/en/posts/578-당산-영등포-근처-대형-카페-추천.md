### Usage Tips

- **Weekends and lunch times fill up quickly**, so for groups of 7 or more, it's safe to visit at least 30 minutes early or call/DM to hold a table in advance.
- For notebooks and presentations, Library or places like Coffee Likors (all floors have outlets) are more convenient.
- House rules for outside food and hourly rates vary by cafe, so it's best to confirm beforehand based on your meeting type.

Enjoy your gathering!
