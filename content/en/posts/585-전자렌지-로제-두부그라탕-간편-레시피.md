## Ingredients

- 1 block of tofu (soft tofu for dressing also works)
- Rose sauce ingredients:
  - 4 tablespoons tomato sauce
  - 100ml heavy cream (or 100ml milk)
  - 1/2 teaspoon minced garlic
  - 1 teaspoon gochujang (Korean red chili paste)
  - 1 teaspoon red chili powder (adjust for spice level)
  - Salt and pepper to taste
- 2-3 slices of cheese (or 1/2 cup pizza cheese/mozzarella cheese)
- Parmesan cheese powder to taste (optional)
- Parsley powder for garnish (optional)

## How to Make

1. Prepare the tofu
   - Cut tofu into flat slices about 1cm thick.
   - Place about 5 tablespoons of water in a microwave-safe bowl, add tofu, cover with plastic wrap, and microwave for about 3 minutes.
   - Remove as much moisture as possible from the tofu (gently press with paper towel or kitchen towel).
2. Make the rose sauce
   - In a microwave-safe bowl, mix tomato sauce, heavy cream (or milk), minced garlic, gochujang, and red chili powder well.
   - Season with salt and pepper.
   - Microwave for about 1 minute 30 seconds, then take out and stir well.
3. Make the tofu gratin
   - Spread the cooked tofu evenly at the bottom of a microwave-safe bowl.
   - Pour the prepared rose sauce evenly over the tofu.
   - Place sliced cheese on top or sprinkle pizza cheese. Reduce the amount of cheese if you prefer less richness.
   - Sprinkle a little parmesan cheese powder (if you have it).
4. Microwave cooking
   - Microwave for 3-4 minutes. The cheese won't brown like in an oven, but it will melt deliciously.
   - Sprinkle parsley powder on top of the finished gratin for garnish (optional).

## Tips

- Remove enough moisture from the tofu for a delicious gratin.
- You can use pizza cheese or sliced cheese instead of parmesan powder according to your preference.
- Adjust the spiciness of the rose sauce with red chili powder.
- Cooking time may vary depending on your microwave model, so adjust while checking the status.
- Tofu cheese has a similar texture to ricotta cheese, so removing as much moisture as possible from the tofu using a paper towel will give you better texture.

This recipe can be easily made with just a microwave and is a nutritious tofu gratin. You can enjoy high-protein tofu dishes deliciously with the combination of cheese and rose sauce.
