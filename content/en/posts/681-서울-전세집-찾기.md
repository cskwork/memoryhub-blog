## Practical Usage Tips

### 1. Taking Advantage of Golden Timing

**Target property upload times**: New listings concentrate at 9-11 AM and 2-4 PM. **Set push notifications** and contact immediately for better competitiveness.

### 2. Negotiating Real Estate Brokerage Fees

Know the **legal brokerage fee cap** and negotiate:

- Up to 50 million won: 0.5%
- 50-100 million won: 0.4%
- Over 100 million won: 0.3%

### 3. Seasonal Strategy Development

**Target off-season (November-January)** for wider selection options. Conversely, **peak season (February-March)** has many listings but intense competition.

### 4. Using a Checklist

- Layer noise (weekend afternoon visits recommended)
