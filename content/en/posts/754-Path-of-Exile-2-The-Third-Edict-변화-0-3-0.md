## 🎯 What Genuinely Changed (Quick Summary)

### 1) Campaign Structure 🗺️

- **Act 4**: Depart Kingsmarch → explore **8 islands in free order**, tons of new monsters, zones, bosses added.
- **Cruel difficulty deleted** → **3-part Interlude** strengthens level 65 transition (more bosses/zones).

### 2) Movement/QoL: **Sprint** 💨

- **Hold dodge key** for full-speed sprint (faster than monsters). But **risk knockdown on hit** → practice entry/exit timing.

### 3) Trading: **Asynchronous (Offline) Trading** 💰

- Act 4 → recruit **Ange** → **Merchant tab** appears in hideout.
- **Auto-matches and completes trades** even when seller is offline. **Buyer pays small fee**.
- Existing **premium tab conversion** supported.

### 4) Combat/Defense Rework ⚔️

- **Deflect**: Convert some evasion into **damage mitigation trigger** (large mitigation on trigger).
- **Can block most attacks** (red alert motions excepted) → parry reliability ↑.
- **Warrior hit buff**, **summons always hit** → stability increased.

### 5) Skill & Gem Expansion 💎

- **Allow duplicate support gems** → utility/ailment/attribute stacking builds possible.
- **Support gem tiers (1~3)** strengthen growth loop.
- **Lineage support gems (~40 types)**: 'Unique-level supports' that change skill mechanics entirely—endgame farming driver.

### 6) Passives & Gear Requirements 📊

- **150+ new notables**, gear **requirement stats -25%** → less path waste, more thematic choices.

### 7) Crafting/Currency/Drop Loop 🪙

- **Upper-tier currencies/essence** appear, **Recombinator** gets **unpredictable guarantee mode** → endgame gear design routes expand.

### 8) Endgame Maps/Bosses 🗡️

- **25 new maps** + **uber variant bosses**, special zone **Rockpools (Goblin King)** etc. → reward curve reset.

### 9) First Dedicated League: **Rise of the Abyssal** 🌑

- Field rift seals → **Abyssal** enhancement → **pit opens** → **Dark Domain** infiltration maximizes rewards.
- Exclusive bosses/crafted items + **Lineage gems major source**.
