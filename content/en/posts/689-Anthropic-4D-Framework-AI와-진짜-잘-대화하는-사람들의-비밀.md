## 1. Background - Are We Ready for the AI Era?

As of 2025, AI has permeated our daily lives. With Anthropic's new AI models capable of continuous work for nearly 7 hours, AI is becoming more than just a tool—it's becoming a 'digital colleague'.

### 🤔 But What's the Reality?

- **"AI doesn't understand what I'm saying"** → Possibly vague explanation
- **"Can I trust AI's answers?"** → Don't know how to verify
- **"If AI does everything, what do I do?"** → Unclear role division

### 📊 3 Levels of AI Utilization

Anthropic presents 3 ways to interact with AI:

| Level | Description | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Automation** | AI automatically completes specific tasks | Automatic email classification |
| **Augmentation** | Humans and AI partner as collaborators | Create proposals together |
| **Agency** | AI works independently as assigned | Personal schedule manager |

## 2. Core Concepts - What's 4D?

> **What is the 4D Framework?**  
> A practical guide for the 4 core competencies to effectively and ethically interact with AI

### 🎯 1. Delegation - "AI Does This, I Do That"

**Core Question: "Should AI do this, or should I?"**

#### ✅ Good Tasks to Delegate to AI:

- Summarize and organize materials
- Draft writing (emails, reports, etc.)
- Information search and collection
- Repetitive tasks
- Brainstorming partner

#### ❌ Tasks I Must Do Myself:

- Final decision-making
- Creative core ideas
- Communication where emotions matter
- Work involving secret information
- Work requiring ethical judgment

**Real Example**:  
"Planning a trip?"

- AI does: Collect destination information, create draft schedule, calculate budget
- I do: Choose final destination, coordinate with companions, make reservations

### 📝 2. Description - "AI Can Read It Too"

**Core Question: "Can AI understand my intention accurately?"**

#### ❌ Poor Description vs ✨ Good Description

**Poor Example**:  
"Write a good email"

**Good Example**:  
"Situation: Requesting a project deadline extension from my boss

- Tone: Respectful but confident
- Content to include: Current progress (80% complete), reason for extension (need quality improvement), new deadline (3 days later)
- Length: 5-7 sentences
- Special request: End with thanks"

#### 🔑 CARE Formula for Effective Descriptions:

- **C**ontext: Explain background
- **A**ction: Specify desired action
- **R**esult: Expected output
- **E**xample: Reference examples

### 🎯️ 3. Discernment - "Should I Trust AI's Answer?"

**Core Question: "Does this output meet my standards?"**

#### ✓ AI Output Checklist:

1. **Accuracy**: Are there factual errors?
2. **Completeness**: Missing anything?
3. **Appropriateness**: Right tone and content for situation?
4. **Consistency**: Does it make sense?
5. **Usefulness**: Is it actually helpful?

**Real Example**:  
Reviewing AI-written cover letter?

- ✅ Verify career facts are accurate
- ✅ Check tone matches company style
- ✅ Evaluate if strengths stand out
- ❌ Look for exaggerated expressions
- ❌ Verify not just generic phrases

### ⚖️ 4. Diligence - "Is It Okay to Use AI This Way?"

**Core Question: "Is it ethical to use AI this way?"**

#### 🛡️ Ethical AI Use Principles:

1. **Transparency**: Disclose AI use when necessary
2. **Verification**: Always confirm important information
3. **Responsibility**: You're ultimately responsible for AI outputs
4. **Fairness**: Ensure no bias or discrimination
5. **Privacy**: Don't input sensitive information to AI

**Real Example**:  
"Writing an assignment with AI?"

- ✅ Use AI as reference tool
- ✅ Add my thoughts and analysis
- ✅ Cite sources (if needed)
- ❌ Submit AI output as-is
- ❌ Try to bypass plagiarism detection

## 3. Practice - Use 4D in Daily Life

### Situation 1: Preparing Important Presentation

**① Delegation Step**

- AI does: Collect related materials, propose presentation structure, create slide drafts
- I do: Decide core message, create storytelling, final review

**② Description Step**  
"Next week's team meeting: 15-minute presentation on 'Improving Customer Satisfaction'

- Audience: 10 marketing team members (including team lead)
- Goal: Present 3 concrete improvement plans
- Tone: Professional but not stiff
- Structure: Problem statement → current analysis → solutions → expected benefits"

**③ Discernment Step**

- Verify data is current
- Check if aligned with company situation
- Evaluate if proposals are feasible

**④ Diligence Step**

- Mention AI helped with ideas
- Clarify need for additional review before implementation

### Situation 2: Learning a Foreign Language

**① Delegation Step**

- AI does: Explain grammar, provide examples, conversation practice partner
- I do: Consistent practice, real-world application, understand cultural context

**② Description Step**  
"Want to learn business English emails

- Current level: 700 TOEIC
- Goal: Comfortable email communication with foreign clients
- Focus areas: Inquiry, proposals, negotiation phrases
- Method: Real situation examples with explanations"

**③ Discernment Step**

- Verify expressions are actually used
- Check tone isn't too formal or casual
- Review cultural appropriateness

**④ Diligence Step**

- Don't rely only on AI translation
- Have native speaker review important business emails

## 4. Best Practices

| Situation | Good Example | Bad Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Information Search** | "List top 5 Korean EV brands in 2024 and their characteristics in a table" | "Tell me about EVs" |
| **Writing** | "Parent notification: friendly and easy to understand, important items in bold" | "Write a notice" |
| **Learning** | "Explain Python for loops using a fruit stand example" | "Teach me Python" |
| **Decision-making** | "Summarize pros and cons, I'll make final choice" | "What should I pick?" |

### 💡 Tips for Becoming a 4D Master

1. **Start Small**: Practice AI collaboration on simple tasks first
2. **Give Feedback**: Tell AI "simpler please," "add example," etc.
3. **Create Templates**: Save frequently used requests as templates
4. **Experiment**: Try different ways to express same request
5. **Document**: Keep records of successful examples
