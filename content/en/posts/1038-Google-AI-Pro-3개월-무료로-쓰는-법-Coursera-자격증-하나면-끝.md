## How-To: From Enrollment to Activating Google AI Pro

**Step 1. Enroll on Coursera**

Visit Coursera (<https://www.coursera.org/professional-certificates/google-ai>) and click "Enroll for free."

A Coursera subscription is required (typically $49/month for US/Canada, varies by country), with a 7-day free trial available.

If you're a Coursera Plus subscriber, you can access it at no additional cost.

**Step 2. Receive Code in Course 2 (AI for Brainstorming and Planning)**

During course study, a "Launch App" button appears. Clicking this button directs you to a page operated by Saritasa, Google's official technology partner, where a unique Google AI Pro trial code is issued.

It's normal if the URL contains "Saritasa"—the actual payment information entry and activation happens on the one.google.com domain.

**Step 3. Activate Google AI Pro**

Register the issued code on the Google One page. A valid payment method must be registered on your Google billing account. Free use for 3 months begins immediately upon activation.

**Step 4. Prevent Automatic Billing**

After 3 months of free access, $19.99 will be charged automatically each month. If you only want the free trial, cancel on the Google Play Store subscription page before the expiration date. You can continue using the service until the free period ends even after cancellation.

Korean users should note one special point. A procedure to agree to the renewal price is required before the trial period ends.

If you don't agree, the subscription won't auto-renew, so Korean users actually have less risk of accidental charges.

To maximize this benefit, you should check a few conditions.

| Item | Details |

| Eligible Countries | 150+ countries including Korea |
| Code Expiration | Until January 1, 2027 |

| Existing Subscribers | If existing trial period remains, it will be cancelled and new trial begins |
| Excluded | Google One add-on subscribers, Ultra plan subscribers, non-admin family plan members, third-party subscribers |

Especially be careful if you're currently using a Google One free trial.

Registering a new trial will cancel your existing one and start a new 3-month period.

If you find your remaining time valuable, it's better to enroll after your current trial ends.
