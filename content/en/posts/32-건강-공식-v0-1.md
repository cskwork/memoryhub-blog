**Mental Health**

Happiness = Situation - Expectations

- Happiness is about living in the present moment rather than the past and future, and feeling what this moment brings.

- Happiness also increases success probability.

- Our initial state is happiness (when a fetus is born)

- Social norms and learned situations create persistent negative and unhappy states, and we come to believe happiness is something that must be achieved through effort.

- What makes me happy is .....

- Finding positive elements in situations where we feel unhappy is important.

- Physical health makes me grow, but psychological suffering is often unnecessary.
