**Asset Check**

- Check current assets and organize by quarter (Current assets - income, expenses. Fixed assets - savings accounts, deposits, funds, savings insurance, etc.)

- Set target assets

- Separate accounts for living expenses, savings accounts, and emergency accounts.

**Investment**

- Monitor market prices for stocks, bonds, real estate, gold, etc., and engage in thematic investing.

- Stay aware of securities and current events.
