- Stop viewing others' kindness in a distorted way. Of course, some people are truly distorted, and sometimes even kind people speak in a skewed manner, but basically, develop good perceptions and a positive outlook toward people. Become someone who can trust others better.

- Hold the mindset that what you give to people you love is not wasteful and that you can give infinitely. Of course, depending on the situation, you also need to save for the future together, but develop a heart as wide as the sea where you can give your all. Become a bigger person, and if after behaving this way for a long time someone doesn't recognize your kindness, it's right to distance yourself from them. Becoming a bigger person should be your top priority.
