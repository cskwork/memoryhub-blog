**Everyone Creates**

14 - Through the most ordinary state of being, people create the experience of reality and construct the world they perceive, so everyone is already walking the creator's path in the most profound way.

**Like an Antenna Receiving Signals**

19 - Every person is like an antenna receiving signals of creative thought.

**(Immerse in Great Works)**

48 - The amount of available information is infinite, but storage space is limited, so we must carefully select the quality of information we let in ... Align our internal standards with greatness.

**Nature is a Teacher**

49 - Among all the great works we can experience, the most absolute and permanent work is nature.

**Look Within**

56 - By focusing on what happens inside—patterns of sensation, emotion, and thought—you can discover abundant material.

**Memory and Subconscious**

57, 58 - There are ways to help approach the deep well inside us ... Fill 5 pages with words that come directly to mind ... The goal is not to think, not to direct content in any way. Write as if spilling out what comes ... Our subconscious stores a lot of high-quality information.

**Create**

69 - Think of creation as free play without rules. There are no right or wrong answers, no good or bad. Starting with that mindset makes it easy to immerse yourself joyfully in the process of making something ... **Perfectionism interferes with fun.**

71 - **Ultimately, the desire to create must be greater than the fear of creating.**

72 - Doubt about the quality of work helps with improvement ... If you have an imperfect work you really like, you might like it less once it's finally perfected.
