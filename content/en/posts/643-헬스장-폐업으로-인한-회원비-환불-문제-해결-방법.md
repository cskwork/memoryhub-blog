Gym closure refund issues are a difficulty faced by many consumers. I'll guide you through the legal steps to resolve this problem.

## Methods You Can Try Immediately

### 1. **Credit Card Installment Withdrawal/Chargeback Rights** (Most Effective)

**Applicable Conditions:**

- Transaction amount is 200,000 won or more and installment period is 3 months or longer
- Withdrawal right: Within 7 days of payment
- Chargeback right: When service is not provided

**How to Apply:**

1. Call the credit card company's customer service immediately
2. Clearly state: "The gym has closed and I cannot receive the service, so I'm exercising my chargeback rights"
3. Apply through the card company's website or mobile app for 'installment withdrawal/chargeback'
4. Send a certified letter to the card company (include date, merchant name, card number, reason for chargeback, etc.)

**Precautions:**

- Already paid installments cannot be refunded, only future installments can be stopped
- Some card companies restrict installments exceeding 3 months for gym businesses, so verify in advance

### 2. **Korea Consumer Agency Damage Relief Application**

**Application Procedure:**

1. Phone consultation at 1372 Consumer Consultation Center (required)
2. Apply for damage relief online within 6 months of consultation
3. Prepare required documents:
   - Contract, receipt, evidence of objections to the business
   - Proof of gym closure
   - Details of refund request

**Online Application Method:**

- Apply online at Korea Consumer Agency website (www.kca.go.kr)
- Online submission is more convenient after confirming damage relief requirements

### 3. **Small Claims Judgment Application**

**Applicable Conditions:**

- Claim amount is 30 million won or less
- Business refuses to refund

**How to Apply:**

1. Apply directly on the Supreme Court's electronic lawsuit website
2. Required costs: Court fee of approximately 10,000 won, delivery fee approximately 50,000 won
3. Required documents: Contract, payment proof, closure documentation

### 4. **Payment Order Application**

**Features:**

- Decision is made without questioning the defendant (gym)
- Enforceable if defendant doesn't object within 2 weeks
- Low cost and simple procedure

**How to Apply:**

1. Apply to the district court with jurisdiction over the defendant's address
2. Prepare payment order application (include party information, claim details and reasons)

## Important Confirmation Points

### 1. **Check Surety Insurance Coverage**

- If the gym has surety insurance, refund is possible through the insurance company
- From May 26, 2025, gyms must notify users when they have surety insurance

### 2. **Refund Calculation Method**

- Consumer Dispute Resolution Standard: Refund amount after deducting days used + 10% compensation on total usage fee

### 3. **Legal Basis**

- 'No refund' clauses are invalid as violations of the Regulation of Terms and Conditions Act
- From May 2025, gyms must notify 14 days before closure

## Additional Recommendations

**Preventive Measures:**

- Always use credit card installment payments for long-term contracts
- Avoid facilities that encourage cash payments
- Carefully consider contracts longer than 1 year

**Key Points Not to Miss:**

- Even if not paid by credit card installment, refunds are possible through consumer agencies or legal procedures
- If the gym files for bankruptcy, you can still exercise your rights as a creditor
- Consider group dispute resolution if there are many victims with similar issues

If the gym owner is refusing refunds, **first check whether you used credit card installment payments**, and if so, **immediately request the card company to exercise chargeback rights**. At the same time, **apply for damage relief at the Korea Consumer Agency**. These two methods are the most effective and fastest solutions.
