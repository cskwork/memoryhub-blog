**Blogger's Note:**  
I want to change jobs but I'm afraid to leave the current stable position.

Or I want to spend more time on self-development in my current job, but I feel like I'm running out of energy and wasting a lot of time.

**Coffee Bean's Response:**   
It seems like work is really tough for you. It could be because of too much work, lack of achievement or compensation, or difficult relationships.

1. If there's too much work, you can talk to your boss and ask to reduce your workload, or you can prioritize yourself and take care of your health even if your work performance drops slightly. However, if unreasonable demands continue, switching jobs might be the only solution.

2. Without adequate compensation for your efforts, motivation disappears and burnout can occur. Even if the company is good, compensation and a sense of achievement for hard work are really important. If the company has growth potential and is going through temporary difficulties, and the company treats you well but you can't afford to improve your market value to move to a better environment, here's a suggestion: Think of yourself as being at the center of the company and believe that you have the power to bring the company back to life. But don't sacrifice yourself excessively or overwork. Instead, create rewards for yourself from your work achievements. It's about finding it from within yourself, not from outside. Give yourself something nice or spend more enjoyable time on weekends.

3. For human relationship difficulties, the answers vary greatly depending on the situation, so I recommend seeking professional help from a psychologist or counselor.

---

Energy depletion seems to require examining how and where you're spending your time. Are you spending too much energy on media? Are you exercising enough? Are you eating well and sleeping soundly? Are you exhausting yourself with excessive worrying? Are you stopping your thoughts and practicing meditation, comforting yourself, and caring for yourself? You need to take good care of yourself to have strength!
