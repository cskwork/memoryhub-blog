As South Korea's Bank of Korea's "Project Hangang" launched in April 2025, the future of financial services we only saw in movies is becoming reality. (Currently in a provisional pause, but it was an experimental attempt that illuminated the future of finance.)

Complex international remittances completed in seconds, 24-hour automated banking, receiving money using just your name instead of account numbers—how is all this possible? After reading this article, you'll clearly understand why banks are investing billions in blockchain and how convenient our daily lives will become.

---

## 1. Background

As the Bank of Korea began CBDC utility testing (Project Hangang) in April 2025, domestic banking sector adoption of blockchain technology is accelerating. Major banks like KB Kookmin Bank and Shinhan Bank are actively investing in blockchain technology development.

**Easy-to-Understand Core Terms**

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **CBDC** | Digital Korean won created by the Bank of Korea (digital version of cash) |
| **Tokenized Deposits** | Technology to convert bank deposits into digital coins |
| **Smart Contracts** | Digital contracts that auto-execute when conditions are met |
| **Digital ID Authentication** | Using easy names instead of complex account numbers |

## 2. Core Concept

> **Banks become computers, working 24 hours automatically**

## 3. Real-World Usage Scenarios Once Implemented

### ① CBDC (Central Bank Digital Currency)

**Now:** Withdraw cash from bank ATM → Pay in cash/card at store

**Future:** Digital won from smartphone → Instant digital payment anywhere

Starting April 2025, you can experience actual digital currency payments through apps from 7 banks including Kookmin Bank and Shinhan Bank.

**Life Changes:**

- Buy coffee at GS25 with digital won
- No currency exchange needed for overseas travel
- Zero bank fees for allowance transfers

### ② Tokenized Deposits

**Now:** Bank account → Internet banking → Request transfer → Wait minutes

**Future:** Smartphone → One tap → Instant transfer complete

Bank deposits become blockchain tokens, enabling 24/7 anytime transfers.

**Convenience Improvements:**

- Real-time transfers on weekends
- International transfers in seconds
- Drastically reduced micro-payment fees

### ③ Smart Contracts (Automated Financial Services)

**Now:** Savings account maturity → Visit bank → Fill out documents → Re-deposit

**Future:** Auto-renewal at maturity, automatic transfer to accounts with better rates

Banks can automatically execute complex financial instructions from customers.

**Automated Services:**

- Automatically distribute salary to living expenses/savings/investments
- Auto-charge from another account if account balance is low
- Auto-convert and send foreign currency when needed

### ④ Digital ID Authentication

**Now:** Remember account number '110-123-456789' impossibly

**Future:** Send money using just a name like 'Hong Gil-dong.Kookmin Bank'

Financial transactions become possible with easy-to-remember names instead of complex account numbers.

**Changes:**

- Enter just a name instead of account number for transfers
- Zero chance of mistaken transfers
- Protect personal information while ensuring convenience

## 4. Pros and Cons Comparison

Technology Advantages Still Need to Address

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **CBDC** | Instant payment, fee savings | Personal data protection plan needed |
| **Tokenized Deposits** | 24-hour service | System stability in progress |
| **Smart Contracts** | Complete automation | Handling complex situations improvements |
| **Digital ID** | Maximized usability | Duplicate name prevention system needed |

## 5. Conclusion

When all these technologies connect, our financial lives will transform completely. Inconveniences like bank hours, fees, and complex procedures will disappear. You'll be able to send and receive money as easily as sending a text message.

With domestic banks accelerating blockchain investments to gain competitive advantage over big tech companies, it looks like these changes will become part of our daily lives faster than expected.

The first opportunity to experience it is the Bank of Korea's digital currency test starting this April. If interested, participate!
