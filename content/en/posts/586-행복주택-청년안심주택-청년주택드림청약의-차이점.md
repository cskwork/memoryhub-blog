## 1. Program Characteristics

### Happiness Housing

- Public rental housing supplied to about 80% of the supply volume to young demographics with vigorous social activities such as newlyweds, youth, and students
- Supplied in areas with convenient public transportation or possible proximity between workplace and residence
- Rental only (no ownership rights)

### Youth Peace Housing

- A Seoul-specific project that supplies youth rental housing in transit-oriented areas and main roads for easy commuting and schooling
- Youth Peace Housing mixes public rental and publicly-supported private rental households within a single complex
- Rental only (no ownership rights)

### Dream Dream Housing Lottery

- A housing purchase support system through subscription contracts
- Upon winning the lottery, loan support for housing purchase is provided
- Housing ownership can be acquired

## 2. Target Population and Eligibility Criteria

### Happiness Housing

- Youth age 19-39, unmarried, and without home ownership
- Income criteria: 100% or less of the previous year's average monthly income of urban workers (120% for single households, 110% for two-person households)
- Asset criteria: Total assets of 273 million won or less, vehicles of 37.08 million won or less

### Youth Peace Housing

- Must be unmarried as of the date of recruitment announcement if applying as youth type
- Income criteria: 120% or less of the previous year's average monthly income of urban workers
- Asset criteria: 254 million won for youth, 337 million won for newlyweds
- Vehicle value: 38.03 million won (as of 2025) or less

### Dream Dream Housing Lottery

- Ages 20-39 without home ownership
- Subscription contract of 1 year or more and actual payment record of 10 million won or more required
- Annual income of 50 million won or less for wage earners, self-employed, and other income earners
- Target housing: Selling price of 600 million won, exclusive area of 85 square meters or less

## 3. Rental/Support Details

### Happiness Housing

- Deposit and rent at 60-80% of market rates
- Residency period: Maximum 6 years for students and youth, 6 years for newlyweds without children, 10 years with children

### Youth Peace Housing

- Supplied at 30-85% of surrounding market prices
- Support available up to 50% of rental deposit, maximum 45 million won (zero interest)

### Dream Dream Housing Lottery

- Loan terms: Interest rates as low as 2.2% (differentiated by income/maturity), maximum repayment period of 40 years, etc.
- Asset formation possible through housing purchase

## 4. Operating Entity

### Happiness Housing

- Supplied nationwide by LH Corporation, SH Corporation, and others

### Youth Peace Housing

- A Seoul-specific program operated by Seoul city

### Dream Dream Housing Lottery

- A national-level financial support program operated by the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport

## Selection Guide

1. If you want to own a home → Dream Dream Housing Lottery
2. If you want to live in rental housing:
   - Want to live in transit-oriented areas in Seoul → Youth Peace Housing
   - Want to live near workplace/school nationwide → Happiness Housing

The biggest difference is that Happiness Housing and Youth Peace Housing provide rental housing, while Dream Dream Housing Lottery is a financial support system for housing purchase.

# Overview of Housing Subscription Reception Centers

## Comparison of Major Housing Subscription Reception Centers

The housing subscription system operates primarily through three platforms, each with distinct characteristics and purposes.

### 1. Applyhome (Korea Real Estate Board)

- **Jurisdiction**: Nationwide private sales, public sales, pre-subscription, etc.
- **Features**: Market-oriented distribution housing integrated reception center
- **Subscription contract**: Must be used, contract becomes null upon winning (cannot maintain non-homeowner points)
- **Website**: applyhome.co.kr

### 2. LH Subscription Plus (Korea Land and Housing Corporation)

- **Jurisdiction**: Nationwide national/permanent/happiness housing, newlywed hope towns, rental housing, etc.
- **Features**: Housing welfare-oriented rental and special public sales
- **Subscription contract**: Varies by type
  - National/happiness/long-term lease rental: Contract not used
  - Public sales/10-year public rental: Contract used, becomes null upon winning
- **Website**: apply.lh.or.kr

### 3. SH Subscription (Housing Information) (Seoul Housing and Urban Development Corporation)

- **Jurisdiction**: Seoul long-term lease 'Shift', youth peace housing, happiness housing, etc.
- **Features**: Seoul-specific housing welfare and public housing
- **Subscription contract**: Varies by type
  - National/long-term lease/happiness housing: Not used
  - Seoul public sales: Used
- **Website**: i-sh.co.kr (Housing Information → Recruitment Notices)

### 4. Other Regional Corporations (GH, iH, etc.)

- **Jurisdiction**: Region-specific public rental/sales
- **Subscription contract**: Same logic as LH/SH
- **Website**: Each corporation's homepage

## Why Platforms Are Separated

1. **Differences in operating entity and funding structure**
   - LH is a national public corporation performing nationwide projects
   - SH/GH and others are local corporations supplying housing for specific regions using local government budgets
2. **Differences in supply objectives**
   - Applyhome: Market-function (sale price, point competition) centered nationwide single center
   - LH/SH: Supply price at 60-80% of market rates, assessment centered on living requirements (income/assets/residence period)
3. **Differences in subscription contract effectiveness**
   - Private/public sales (Applyhome): Contract becomes null upon winning
   - Rental housing (LH/SH): Contract not used or remains valid even if used
   - Public sales/10-year public rental: Contract becomes null
4. **Differences in reception method and processing speed**
   - Applyhome: Standardized procedures and schedule
   - LH/SH: Continuous reception or longer review stages (4-6 weeks required)

## Practical Usage Tips

| Situation | Center to Check First | Check Points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seoul resident, youth/newlywed rental | SH Subscription + LH Subscription | Can support both places, rental protects contract |
| Gyeonggi/Incheon resident, newlywed hope town | LH Subscription | Confirm if sales-conversion type or long-term rental type |
| Top priority for homeownership, 60+ points | Applyhome | Upon winning, contract becomes null/non-homeowner status changes |
| High possibility of housing situation change | LH/SH rental | Contract preservation, minor penalties upon mid-exit |

## Key Summary

**"For private/public sales use 'Applyhome', for housing-welfare oriented rental or specific public sales use 'LH/SH and other corporation websites'."**

Understanding this structure makes it easy to determine which platform to use through the supply entity, region, and whether the subscription contract is used.
