#### ***1 Ingredients Preparation***

*(Based on 4 servings, measured with rice spoons)*

Pork for stew 200g   
(Boiling water 600ml + salted shrimp paste 2 spoons)  
Aged kimchi 400g  
Leek 2/3 pieces, green chili pepper 2 pieces, tofu 1/2 block  
?Seasoning  
Soy sauce 1 spoon, red chili powder 1.5 spoons  
Minced garlic 1 spoon  
Sugar 1-2 spoons  
(Adjust according to the sourness of the kimchi)

#### ***2 Making Pork Broth***

![](/images/45-%EC%9A%94%EB%A6%AC-%EA%B9%80%EC%B9%98%EC%B0%8C%EA%B0%9C-%EB%A7%8C%EB%93%A4%EA%B8%B0-v0-1/img_2.png)

![](/images/45-%EC%9A%94%EB%A6%AC-%EA%B9%80%EC%B9%98%EC%B0%8C%EA%B0%9C-%EB%A7%8C%EB%93%A4%EA%B8%B0-v0-1/img_3.png)

Pour 600ml of water into 200g of pork for stew and simply boil it. (No need to fry)

Any cut of pork works.  
Usually use front leg or neck meat.

An important ingredient goes in here.  
Add ***2 spoons of salted shrimp paste*** with a rice spoon.  
*(Can be substituted with liquid fish sauce)*

When it comes to a rolling boil, reduce to medium-low heat and  
***boil for 20-30 minutes***.  
The stew tastes good when the pork fat comes out sufficiently.

#### ***3 Preparing Stew Ingredients***

![](/images/45-%EC%9A%94%EB%A6%AC-%EA%B9%80%EC%B9%98%EC%B0%8C%EA%B0%9C-%EB%A7%8C%EB%93%A4%EA%B8%B0-v0-1/img_4.png)

![](/images/45-%EC%9A%94%EB%A6%AC-%EA%B9%80%EC%B9%98%EC%B0%8C%EA%B0%9C-%EB%A7%8C%EB%93%A4%EA%B8%B0-v0-1/img_5.png)

While the pork broth is finishing,  
let's prepare the ingredients that go in.  
First, the aged kimchi!

Cut it roughly into bite-sized pieces with scissors.

Slice the leek and green chili pepper thinly,  
and cut the tofu into bite-sized pieces and prepare.

#### ***4 Cooking with Ingredients***

![](/images/45-%EC%9A%94%EB%A6%AC-%EA%B9%80%EC%B9%98%EC%B0%8C%EA%B0%9C-%EB%A7%8C%EB%93%A4%EA%B8%B0-v0-1/img_6.png)

![](/images/45-%EC%9A%94%EB%A6%AC-%EA%B9%80%EC%B9%98%EC%B0%8C%EA%B0%9C-%EB%A7%8C%EB%93%A4%EA%B8%B0-v0-1/img_7.png)

![](/images/45-%EC%9A%94%EB%A6%AC-%EA%B9%80%EC%B9%98%EC%B0%8C%EA%B0%9C-%EB%A7%8C%EB%93%A4%EA%B8%B0-v0-1/img.png)

Replenish the water by the amount that has reduced.

Add the main ingredient aged kimchi

Here add **1 spoon of soy sauce, 1.5 spoons of red chili powder**  
**1 spoon of minced garlic** to season.

To remove the bitter taste of kimchi,  
also add **1-2 spoons of sugar**.  
*(Adjust according to the sourness of the kimchi)*

**Boil for 20-30 minutes** on medium-low heat for a long time.

![](/images/45-%EC%9A%94%EB%A6%AC-%EA%B9%80%EC%B9%98%EC%B0%8C%EA%B0%9C-%EB%A7%8C%EB%93%A4%EA%B8%B0-v0-1/img_1.png)

Finish the well-simmered kimchi jjigae  
with green chili pepper, leek, and tofu.
