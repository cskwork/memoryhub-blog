**Main Ingredients:**

Pork belly (samgyeopsal) 200g

Kimchi (aged kimchi) 2 paper cups

Green onion (scallion) a small amount

**Broth:**

Dried kelp (dasima) 1 sheet (dasima tea bag can also be used)

Anchovy (myulchi) a handful

**Sauce:**

Minced garlic 1

Sugar 1

1. Prepare the broth separately

2. Cook the meat first

3. Stir-fry with kimchi to remove sourness (add sugar at this time) --> About 10 minutes until the color becomes transparent

4. Pour in the broth and add minced garlic, green onion, and other ingredients

5. If the broth is bland, cook longer; if too salty, add more broth (don't add kimchi juice directly as it becomes pasty)

6. Enjoy your meal!
